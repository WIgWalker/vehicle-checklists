Daily Vehicle Checklist

Complete one day at a time. Every inspection item can be checked off. Working entries save automatically on this device.
Ready
Apple devices: tap Save to iCloud / Files. On iPhone or iPad, choose Save to Files → iCloud Drive → Vehicle Checklists → Completed. The file name is automatically created from the vehicle, driver, day and date.
Saving your completed checklist
  1. Tap Save to iCloud / Files.
  2. In the Apple share sheet, tap Save to Files.
  3. Select iCloud Drive.
  4. Open Vehicle Checklists, then Completed.
  5. Tap Save.
If you do not already have the folders, create them once in the Files app. Your completed checklist will then be available on your iPhone, iPad and Mac using the same Apple Account.

Saved Completed Checklists

This list is a local history on the device. The actual completed documents are saved through Apple Files/iCloud Drive when you use the Save button.
